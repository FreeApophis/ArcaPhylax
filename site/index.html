Local-first · open source

Your documents and media,
kept on your own computer.

ArcaPhylax is a desktop app for collecting, indexing and finding your documents, images and videos. Everything stays local, in a database on your machine. Plugins add whatever else you need.

Get started Browse plugins

What it does

Documents

Drop folders and files onto the app. ArcaPhylax indexes them in the background so you can find them again.

Images and videos

Browse your media in one place and find similar images and duplicates.

Tags

Organise anything with your own tags instead of a fixed folder structure.

Private by design

No account and no cloud. Your library is a SQLite database in your user profile.

Extend it with plugins

Plugins add new pages to the app. Install them from the Plugins page inside ArcaPhylax: it lists everything on the plugin page that runs on your computer. Every package is signed, and ArcaPhylax only installs packages from publishers it trusts.

Scanner

Scan documents from TWAIN, WIA and eSCL scanners and save them as PDF or images.

Nutrition

Track daily calories and body weight per person, with charts of your progress.

Fractal

A GPU-accelerated Mandelbrot explorer with pan and zoom (Windows).
